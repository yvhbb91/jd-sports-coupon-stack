## Free shipping: know which threshold applies to you

There are two shipping thresholds floating around JD Sports' official pages, and they're easy to confuse.

The standing policy is **free standard shipping on orders over $75** (U.S. residential and commercial addresses). Under that, it's a flat **$8.99**. The minimum is calculated *after* discounts, coupons, and STATUS rewards are applied, and before tax. Alaska and Hawaii don't get the free-shipping offer, and JD Sports won't ship to Puerto Rico, Guam, APO/FPO boxes, or international addresses at all. P.O. Boxes only qualify via USPS Economy Shipping.

The "$100 free shipping" offer on the coupon page is a separate listed deal — and honestly, for most orders the standing $75 threshold is the better number. If your cart is at $80, you already qualify for free shipping under the standard policy; you don't need the coupon-page offer at all.

The sleeper deal here is the own-brand rule: any single item from JD Sports' in-house brands — **Supply & Demand, Sonneti, Pink Soda, DAILYSZN, and Hoodrich** — ships free once that one item is over $15. Note it's per-item pricing, not cart total. A $16 Hoodrich hoodie ships free even if it's the only thing in your order. If you're buying for kids who outgrow everything in a season, the own-brand section plus the 2-for-$40 kids' deal covers a lot of ground cheaply.
